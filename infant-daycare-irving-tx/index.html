Infant Daycare & Baby Care in Irving, TX

At Shady Grove Learning Center, our infant daycare program provides a safe, peaceful and warm sensory environment designed for your baby’s critical first year of development.

Our infant room follows your baby’s own rhythm. Teachers match your home routine for feeding, sleeping and diapering, and you receive frequent updates throughout the day. We are a 3-Star Texas Rising Star center open Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

Infant Nursery Highlights:

  • Customized Care Plans: We follow your baby’s home routine for feeding, sleeping, and diapering.
  • Tummy Time & Motor Skill Growth: Daily interactive play encouraging rolling, crawling, and reaching.
  • Sanitized & Safe Sleeping Areas: Individual assigned cribs following strict SIDS safety guidelines.
  • Daily Parent Communication: Frequent updates on feedings, nap lengths, and diaper changes.

Infant Daycare FAQ

What ages does the infant program at Shady Grove Learning Center serve?

+

Our infant program cares for babies from 6 weeks to 12 months old in Irving, TX.

How do you keep infants safe while they sleep?

+

Each infant has an individual assigned crib in a sanitized sleeping area, and we follow strict safe sleep (SIDS) guidelines.

Will teachers follow my baby's feeding and nap schedule?

+

Yes. We follow your baby's home routine for feeding, sleeping and diapering, and we share frequent updates on feedings, nap lengths and diaper changes.

Are meals included, and is subsidized child care accepted?

+

Yes. Breakfast, a warm lunch and healthy snacks are included (after-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack). We also accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 for current availability.

How do I tour the center and enroll?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour form on this page. Enrollment is three steps: tour the center, complete the enrollment packet with Texas health forms and immunization records, and start your child's first day. Hours are Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060.
