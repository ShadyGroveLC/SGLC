After-School Program & Summer Camp in Irving, TX

Looking for reliable after-school child care in Irving, TX? Our After-Schoolers Program provides school-age children with a structured environment to complete homework, engage in recreational sports, and enjoy healthy afternoon meals.

Children ages 5 to 12 are welcome after school until 6:30 PM, with full-day holiday and summer camp care during school breaks.

After-School Program Benefits:

  • Homework Assistance: Dedicated quiet study hall with teachers available for guidance.
  • Sports & Outdoor Games: Active playground games promoting team building and physical health.
  • Holiday & Summer Camps: Full-day care options available during school breaks and summer vacation.
  • Healthy Afternoon Snacks: A nutritious snack is provided every afternoon.

After-School Care FAQ

What ages does the after-school program serve?

+

Our After-School Program serves school-age children ages 5 to 12 in Irving, TX.

Do you offer care during holidays and summer?

+

Yes. Full-day care is available during school holidays and breaks, and we offer a summer camp program.

Are meals included, and is subsidized child care accepted?

+

Yes. Breakfast, a warm lunch and healthy snacks are included (after-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack). We also accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 for current availability.

How do I tour the center and enroll?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour form on this page. Enrollment is three steps: tour the center, complete the enrollment packet with Texas health forms and immunization records, and start your child's first day. Hours are Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060.
