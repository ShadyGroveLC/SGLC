About Shady Grove

A Safe, Caring Home Away From Home in Irving

Located conveniently at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Shady Grove Learning Center is dedicated to cultivating a rich educational foundation for children in Irving and surrounding communities.

We understand the demands on working parents, which is why we offer extended hours from 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Our balanced daily routine mixes hands-on learning, physical play, emotional development, and healthy meals to support your child’s growth at every stage.

A warm, soothing environment where infants receive personal care, gentle stimulation, tummy time, and sensory exploration from attentive caregivers.

Toddler Discovery

Encouraging independence through guided play, vocabulary growth, music, storytime, art projects, and early social interaction with peers.
