Parent FAQ

Day Care & Child Care Questions in Irving, TX

Quick answers about hours, ages, meals, enrollment and our 3-Star Texas Rising Star rating.

What are the hours at Shady Grove Learning Center in Irving, TX?

+

Shady Grove Learning Center is open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM. These extended hours are designed to support working parents in Irving, TX.

What ages does Shady Grove Learning Center serve?

+

We care for children from 6 weeks old through 12 years old: Infants (6 weeks to 12 months), Toddlers (12 to 24 months), 2-Year-Olds, 3-Year-Olds (preschool), and After-Schoolers (ages 5 to 12), including holiday and summer camp care.

What does it mean to be a 3-Star Texas Rising Star child care center?

+

Texas Rising Star is a quality rating system from the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC). A 3-Star Texas Rising Star facility, like Shady Grove Learning Center, meets quality standards that go beyond minimum state licensing requirements.

Are meals included in tuition?

+

Yes. Meals are included: breakfast, a warm lunch, and healthy snacks are served daily. After-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack.

Does Shady Grove Learning Center accept subsidized child care?

+

Yes. We accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 to ask about current availability.

How do I enroll my child?

+

Enrollment takes three steps: (1) schedule a facility tour, (2) complete the enrollment packet with standard Texas health forms and immunization records, and (3) begin your child's first day. Full-time and part-time options are available for all age groups.

Where is Shady Grove Learning Center located?

+

We are located at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060, in South Irving near major commute routes, with safe drop-off and pick-up access.

Do you help with potty training for 2-year-olds?

+

Yes. Our 2-Year-Old Program includes potty training support, working in partnership with parents to match the approach used at home.

Do you offer after-school care and summer camp in Irving, TX?

+

Yes. Our After-School Program serves children ages 5 to 12 with homework assistance, sports and outdoor games, and a nutritious snack, with care available until 6:30 PM. Full-day care is also offered during holidays, school breaks, and summer camp.

What safety practices does the infant program follow?

+

Infants have individual assigned cribs in sanitized sleeping areas following safe sleep (SIDS) guidelines. Teachers follow each baby's home routine for feeding, sleeping, and diapering and share daily updates with parents. The center also uses secure keypad entry.

How do I schedule a tour or request rates?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour or contact forms on this page to request a private tour and a detailed rate packet.
