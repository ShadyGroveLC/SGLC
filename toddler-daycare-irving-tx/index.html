Toddler Child Care & Discovery in Irving, TX

Our toddler classroom encourages curiosity and early independence as children explore walking, talking, singing, and social interactions under the supervision of certified early childhood teachers in Irving, TX.

Toddlers enjoy first words, motor coordination, songs, storytime and secure socialization every day. Breakfast, a warm lunch and snacks are included, and care is available Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

Toddler Curriculum Focus Areas:

  • First Words & Language: Storytelling, songs, and fingerplays designed to build active vocabulary.
  • Sensory & Fine Motor Skills: Blocks, finger painting, stackable toys, and shape sorters.
  • Outdoor Physical Play: Safe playgrounds tailored for toddler steps and balance.
  • Songs, Storytime & Socialization: Group activities that build coordination and secure friendships.

Toddler Daycare FAQ

What ages does the toddler program serve?

+

Our toddler program serves children from 12 to 24 months old in Irving, TX.

What do toddlers learn at Shady Grove Learning Center?

+

Toddlers build first words and vocabulary through stories, songs and fingerplays, strengthen fine motor skills with blocks, finger painting, stackable toys and shape sorters, and enjoy safe outdoor play.

Are meals included, and is subsidized child care accepted?

+

Yes. Breakfast, a warm lunch and healthy snacks are included (after-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack). We also accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 for current availability.

How do I tour the center and enroll?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour form on this page. Enrollment is three steps: tour the center, complete the enrollment packet with Texas health forms and immunization records, and start your child's first day. Hours are Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060.
