3-Year-Old Preschool & Day Care in Irving, TX

Our 3-Year-Old Preschool Program sets the foundation for pre-kindergarten literacy, phonics, mathematics, science discovery, and independent problem-solving skills in a structured classroom setting.

Our curriculum follows the Texas Early Learning Guidelines. Breakfast, a warm lunch and a snack are included, and care is available Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

Preschool Learning Objectives:

  • Phonics & Early Writing: Letter recognition, name tracing, and vocabulary expansion.
  • Math & Logic Centers: Number sequencing, patterns, counting objects, and sorting shapes.
  • STEM Exploration: Hands-on science activities, nature observations, and building challenges.
  • Kindergarten Prep Foundations: Structured routines that build independence and school readiness.

3-Year-Old Preschool FAQ

What does the 3-year-old preschool program teach?

+

Preschoolers work on phonics and early writing, number sequencing and patterns, hands-on STEM exploration, and independent problem-solving to prepare for pre-kindergarten and kindergarten.

Which curriculum does the preschool follow?

+

The 3-year-old program follows the Texas Early Learning Guidelines.

Are meals included, and is subsidized child care accepted?

+

Yes. Breakfast, a warm lunch and healthy snacks are included (after-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack). We also accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 for current availability.

How do I tour the center and enroll?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour form on this page. Enrollment is three steps: tour the center, complete the enrollment packet with Texas health forms and immunization records, and start your child's first day. Hours are Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060.
