2-Year-Old Day Care & Potty Training in Irving, TX

Two-year-olds are full of energy and creativity! Our 2-Year-Old Daycare Program balances structured early learning routines, artistic expression, social interaction, and supportive toilet training partnerships with parents.

Children build language, learn to share and take turns, and explore colors, shapes and counting. Meals are included and care is available Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at our 3-Star Texas Rising Star center.

Key Program Features for 2-Year-Olds:

  • Potty Training Partnership: Dedicated support and encouragement matching your home toilet training strategy.
  • Early Social Skills: Learning to share, take turns, and express feelings verbally in group circles.
  • Colors, Shapes & Counting: Interactive learning centers that teach color sorting, basic counting, and spatial awareness.
  • Creative Art & Language Building: Painting, songs and conversation that grow vocabulary and self-expression.

2-Year-Old Program FAQ

Do you help with potty training for 2-year-olds?

+

Yes. Our 2-Year-Old Program includes potty training support that partners with parents and matches the approach you use at home.

What will my 2-year-old learn?

+

Two-year-olds practice sharing, taking turns and expressing feelings in group circles, and explore colors, shapes and basic counting through hands-on learning centers, art and language-building activities.

Are meals included, and is subsidized child care accepted?

+

Yes. Breakfast, a warm lunch and healthy snacks are included (after-school children receive a nutritious afternoon snack). We also accept subsidized child care through the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and Child Care Services. Call (972) 986-9351 for current availability.

How do I tour the center and enroll?

+

Call (972) 986-9351, email shadygrovelearn@gmail.com, or use the Schedule a Tour form on this page. Enrollment is three steps: tour the center, complete the enrollment packet with Texas health forms and immunization records, and start your child's first day. Hours are Monday-Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM at 3200 W Shady Grove Rd, Irving, TX 75060.
